How the mapping works

The calculator works from a known matching point: a Blender frame and the corresponding zero-based source-video frame. The source time is then adjusted by the elapsed Blender frame time, including any manual offset.

When this helps

If your reference uses 30 fps and your Blender scene uses 24 fps, video frame numbers should not advance at the same rate as Blender frame numbers. Use the calculator to identify the video frame to inspect at a specific Blender frame.

Important limitations

This assumes constant frame rates and standard video playback. Variable-frame-rate footage, speed ramps, or edits can require separate per-shot anchors or a converted constant-frame-rate reference. The tool does not change your Blender file.

Blender frames usually start at 1, while video frame indices generally start at 0. Pick a clearly identifiable matching event for your anchor.
