How the crop works

This calculator uses a “fill” crop: it preserves the maximum possible rectangle of the requested aspect ratio inside your original frame without stretching the image. The remainder falls outside the chosen frame. It assumes the crop is centred; actual composition might require shifting the crop toward your subject.

Crop is not the same as resizing

Cropping removes source pixels. Resizing scales the retained region to a new output resolution. For example, cropping 1920×1080 footage to 9:16 yields approximately 608×1080 source pixels before scaling. Enlarging that to 1080×1920 does not create new detail.

Blender camera tip

If you change the render resolution from landscape to vertical, also check the camera's composition, safe areas, lens settings and character positions. A resolution change alone can place a character outside the output frame.
