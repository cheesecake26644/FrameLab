Estimated wall time4.7 hours

This assumes equally fast workers, perfect parallelism, no queue or startup delays, and constant per-frame cost. Actual Blender render times vary considerably.

Estimate before launching a long render

Render a few representative frames first. A single easy frame rarely captures the cost of smoke, volumetrics, reflections or complex motion blur later in a scene. Use an average based on several frames if the workload changes.

What about multiple machines?

When several render workers can process frames independently, total wall-clock time may fall roughly in proportion to the number of workers. However, workers may have different speeds, and setup, I/O, failed frames and dependencies reduce that benefit. This calculator deliberately uses a simple optimistic estimate.

Quick ways to avoid wasted renders

Render a low-resolution preview, check the camera and animation, verify output paths and frame ranges, and only then switch to final settings. Save a project version you can reliably reopen before committing to an overnight render.
