Why organize by frame ranges?

A shot list helps you identify what needs revision without repeatedly scrubbing through a long project. Using frame ranges is also less ambiguous than rough timestamps when your shot begins halfway through a second.

Inclusive frame ranges

This planner treats start and end frames as included. A shot running from frame 178 through frame 202 therefore contains 202 − 178 + 1 = 25 frames. That convention is useful when marking render ranges and shot boundaries, but remember that some editing tools use exclusive end points.

Where is your data stored?

Your shot list lives in your browser's local storage, not on our servers. Clearing site data or switching browsers may erase it. Export your CSV regularly so you retain a separate copy. No account is required.
