How the eight poses fit together

A two-step cycle commonly starts with Contact A, then passes through Down A, Passing A and Up A. The other leg then repeats those stages. The ninth row closes the loop at the next Contact A. In a 24-frame cycle, this tool spaces the major poses three frames apart as an initial timing scaffold.

Why timing is only a starting point

Equal pose spacing is convenient for blockout, but believable walking depends on character mass, tempo, anatomy, and intent. A cautious walk spends a different amount of time in support than a brisk, confident walk. Adjust individual keyframes rather than treating this table as a final animation.

Keep your Blender animation editable

Set meaningful Contact, Down, Passing and Up key poses first. Then refine the Graph Editor curves, compensate for pelvis and torso rotation, and add breakdown poses where the motion calls for them. An IK foot control is useful during stance; FK can be useful for free-moving arm arcs. Pose-preserving switching matters more than which control mode is considered “best.”

Start with camera and timing alignment, then character poses, then arcs and secondary movement. Adding more keyframes before solving those foundations usually creates more cleanup.
