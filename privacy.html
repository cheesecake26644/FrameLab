Site policy

Privacy & data handling.

A clear explanation of what this website stores, and how advertising may change that in the future.

Last updated: 8 November 2026. This policy applies to the FrameLab website as supplied. The operator must review it if hosting, analytics, advertising or other services change.

Data used by the tools

The calculators run entirely in your browser. The shot-list planner saves shot names and frame ranges in browser local storage so they remain available after a page reload. This information is not sent to a FrameLab server by the supplied site code. You can erase it using the tool's “Clear all” button or your browser's site-data controls.

Hosting logs

Your hosting provider may process standard technical information, such as IP address and request metadata, to deliver the website and protect its infrastructure. Consult the chosen hosting provider's privacy terms for details.

Advertising

Advertising is disabled in the provided website until the operator explicitly configures an approved provider. If advertisements are enabled later, the provider may use cookies, device identifiers and other information to serve, measure or personalize ads, subject to applicable legal requirements and consent choices. This policy must be updated with the provider's disclosures before activation.

Analytics and third parties

The supplied site includes no third-party analytics scripts, tracking pixels, external fonts or advertising scripts while advertising is disabled. If the site operator adds such services, the disclosures and consent process must be updated accordingly.

Your choices

You can use all tools without creating an account. You may clear local storage or block browser storage through your browser settings, although your shot list will then not persist.

Contact

For questions about privacy, contact the site operator at Contact details to be added before launch.

Before launching ads, update this policy for the actual provider, region-specific privacy requirements and any required consent management platform. This is a starting policy, not legal advice.
