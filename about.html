About

Practical tools for making better frames.

FrameLab is an independent collection of simple utilities and learning resources for animators and video editors.

Why FrameLab exists

Animation and editing involve more frame math than anyone signed up for. Translating frame numbers into time, laying out a walk cycle, tracking a shot's frame range and deciding what a crop removes are small tasks, but they are repeated constantly. FrameLab brings them into one quick, free toolkit.

What makes it useful

Every calculator runs directly in your browser without an account. The shot planner stores its data locally and can export CSV files. Guides focus on diagnosing real production problems rather than promoting a particular software package.

Advertising and independence

FrameLab may display clearly identified advertisements in the future to help keep the tools free. Advertising is not active by default, and any future ads will be distinguished from editorial content. We do not promise or guarantee advertising earnings to anyone.
